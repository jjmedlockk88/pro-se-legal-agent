# Filing Quality Gate

No document is filing-ready until every item below is checked:

- Correct court, division, caption, case number, parties, and capacity.
- Correct current rule, statute, and jurisdiction.
- Every factual assertion has a record cite, exhibit, or is clearly labeled allegation.
- Every legal citation was opened and verified; no fabricated or placeholder citations remain.
- Requested relief is legally available and specific.
- Service, certificate, exhibits, page limits, formatting, signature, and local rules checked from current official sources.
- Confidential information redacted; sealed-material procedure considered.
- Criminal/civil overlap, privilege, immunity, limitations, Heck, abstention, and sanctions risk reviewed.
- The draft was compared against the docket and prior orders.
- User personally reviews and explicitly approves before filing or service.

End every draft with:

`DO NOT FILE YET — HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`

followed by unresolved facts, unverified authorities, missing exhibits, and deadline risks.
