# Research and Source-Verification Skill

## Source hierarchy

1. Court orders, docket entries, statutes, rules, and opinions from official sources.
2. PACER/RECAP/CourtListener copies cross-checked against official records when possible.
3. Government agency policies and public-record responses.
4. Treatises, legal-aid guides, and secondary sources only as orientation.
5. Search snippets and AI output are leads, never authority.

## Research record

For each proposition store: query, source URL, court, case/statute/rule, date accessed, quoted holding or text, pinpoint/page/paragraph, jurisdiction, negative treatment/check, and applicability to the user's facts.

## Agent research loop

- Search broad, then narrow by `Nye County`, `Nevada`, `District of Nevada`, and `Ninth Circuit`.
- Open the source; never cite a title or snippet alone.
- Check current version and amendments.
- Locate adverse authority and procedural distinctions.
- Quote only what the source actually says.
- Mark unresolved items `VERIFY BEFORE FILING`.

## Free-source suggestions

CourtListener/RECAP; official Nevada Legislature NRS pages; Nevada Supreme Court and Nevada judiciary pages; District of Nevada and Ninth Circuit sites; official Nye County/court pages; PACER where needed. Respect robots.txt, terms, rate limits, and authentication. Do not scrape or bypass access controls.
